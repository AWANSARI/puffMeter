Privacy

PuffyMeter is a cosmetic wellness tracker. It is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose anything.

Your face stays yours

  • Face analysis runs entirely in your browser. Camera frames are never sent anywhere.
  • Photos and scores are stored on your device: in browser storage and, if you choose, in a folder you pick or a backup file you save.
  • If you connect Google Drive, PuffyMeter uploads each day's photo (JPG) and score file (JSON) to the single folder you choose, using the drive.file permission. It can't see the rest of your Drive.
  • There's no PuffyMeter database, and no ads, analytics or trackers. Google sign-in runs in small stateless functions: your Google refresh token is kept only in an encrypted cookie in your own browser, and Drive requests are passed straight through to Google.

Google user data

PuffyMeter's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your name and email are shown in the app only; they're never sold or shared.

Your controls

Export your data or delete everything from Profile › Your data. Disconnecting Drive or deleting PuffyMeter data doesn't delete the files in your Drive folder; you can remove those in Google Drive.

Terms

PuffyMeter is provided as is, for personal, non-medical use. Scores are estimates that vary with lighting, expression and daily changes.
